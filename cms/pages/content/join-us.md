## Information

This application is for current 9th and 10th graders interested in joining HackGwinnett’s executive board. Current 11th graders will be considered, but underclassmen are preferred.

There are no formal positions at HackGwinnett; we all work towards our mission—promoting computer science and technology education in Georgia—in an equal fashion, utilizing our own unique skill sets. You won’t find formal position descriptions on the application or elsewhere.

Please be honest and thoughtful in your responses. We are not looking for the applicant with the most technical experience, nor will we automatically select those who have won hackathons. Both those things are nice to have, but above all, we want unique team members that are interested in CS and stand out from the crowd.

## Instructions

Once you have completed the [application](/go/hgta27app) in its entirety, upload it as a PDF to the appropriate field on the [form](/go/hgta27form) using the following naming convention:

```
Last_First_HGTA27.pdf
```

e.g., `Lovelace_Ada-Lee_HGTA27.pdf` (for the name “Ada-Lee Lovelace”)

If you’re attaching any optional, external documents—such as a resume, recommendation, or cover letter—upload it to the appropriate field on the form as:

```
Last_First_DocumentDescription_HGTA27_D.pdf
```

e.g., `Lovelace_Ada-Lee_Resume_HGTA27_D.pdf`

Contact us at [hackgwinnett@gmail.com](mailto:hackgwinnett@gmail.com) or DM us on [Instagram (@hackgwinnett)](/go/instagram) if you have any questions. You must submit your application to the form on time for us to consider your application.

Thank you for your interest in becoming part of HackGwinnett’s leadership.
