Hello, world!

HackGwinnett Team Applications are open for SY2027–28! If you're a current underclassman who cares about computer science and wants to help bring tech education to students across Georgia, we want to hear from you.

## No titles, just teamwork

There are no formal positions at HackGwinnett. We all work toward the same mission (promoting computer science and technology education in Georgia), and each of us brings our own skills to the table. That means you won't find position descriptions anywhere.

## You don't need to be a hackathon champion

We aren't looking for the applicant with the most technical experience, and we won't automatically pick people who have won hackathons. Those are nice to have, but above all, we want unique team members who are interested in CS and stand out from the crowd. Be honest and thoughtful in your answers, and let us see who YOU are.

## How to apply

1. Get the [application document](/go/hgta27app) (select "Use Template" to edit in Docs).
2. Complete it, then save it as a PDF named `Last_First_HGTA27.pdf` (e.g., `Lovelace_Ada-Lee_HGTA27.pdf`).
3. Upload it to the [application form](/go/hgta27form). Optional extras like a resume or recommendation go in their own field as `Last_First_[description]_HGTA27_D.pdf`.

> Read the full instructions and check out the timeline (including _due dates_!) on our [Join Us page](/about/join-us). Applications must be submitted on time to be considered.

Questions? Email us at hackgwinnett@gmail.com or DM us on [Instagram](/go/instagram) (@hackgwinnett).

We can't wait to meet you!

HackGwinnett Team
